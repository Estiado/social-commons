# Photographic book and painted fore-edge

The active book plate is a complete photographic composite, produced by
`scripts/build-book-photo.py`. The supplied photograph is retained unchanged at
`design/book/open-book-reference.jpg`. It contains no generated writing or additional
painted-edge copy. Previously drawn paper planes have been removed.

## Complete photographic book

- Source: image supplied by the user, original filename `427d16c7a2b47bf8a59833cf1755eb13.jpg`.
- The supplied original includes a source-account mark, preserved in the canonical source.
  An originating webpage and separate licence were not supplied; this asset is not claimed
  to be CC0 or covered by the software licences.
- Modification: select the connected book silhouette, remove the surrounding background,
  retouch original ink within the reading areas using a local paper filter, composite
  painted edges over the photographed paper blocks, and add a contact shadow.
- The whole visible sheet shape, gutter, paper layers and ageing come from this photograph.
  No cover style is a selection requirement. The adaptation is confined to the preview branch.

## Dunhuang painting

- Photographer: Gary Todd, Xinzheng, China.
- Work: Tang Mural from Dunhuang (9923168493), captioned as a late-Tang mural.
- Primary source and licence record:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tang_Mural_from_Dunhuang_(9923168493).jpg
- Licence: CC0, verified from Wikimedia imageinfo metadata on 2026-10-30.
- Modification: crop the painted foliage, excluding labels; normalize exposure, adapt
  the crop into a continuous colour band, and multiply it by the photographed paper
  layers. This adaptation is not a restoration or a documentary reproduction of the mural.

## Material reference only

The continuous painted-edge layout was studied on the real bookseller's product page:
https://detail.youzan.com/show/goods?alias=271ht30cesmzv35&from_source=gbox_seo
(`敦煌日课` 珍藏刷边版, 中信出版). Its promotional photographs are not shipped as assets.

The final interface text is live HTML. The painted edge intentionally has no copy.
`book-photo.webp` is the complete plate; `book-photo-left.webp` is the responsive single leaf.
